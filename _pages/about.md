I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Optimization Theory at [Uppsala University](https://www.uu.se/en), hosted by [Prashant Singh](https://www.it.uu.se/katalog/prasi372?lang=en). I work on deterministic and stochastic optimization algorithms with applications to machine learning and operations research. I have also been awarded a WASP postdoctoral scholarship from the Wallenberg Foundation for a two-year postdoctoral stay at Inria Paris, hosted by [Francis Bach](https://www.di.ens.fr/~fbach/).

I completed my PhD in Mathematical Statistics at Umeå University in February 2026 as a WASP doctoral researcher, supervised by [Alp Yurtsever](https://www.umu.se/en/staff/alp-yurtsever/). In my thesis, *From Accelerated First-Order Methods to Structured Nonconvex Optimization: Analysis and Perspectives*, I studied accelerated optimization methods through their continuous-time behaviour and structured nonconvex problems through difference-of-convex programming, with applications to machine learning. During my PhD, I was a research intern at Pendulum Systems and a visiting researcher at the University of Edinburgh and the Technical University of Munich. Before that, I received my M.Sc. in Telecommunication Engineering and my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology.

**Research interests:** convex and nonconvex optimization, difference-of-convex programming, bilevel programming, accelerated algorithms, stochastic methods in machine learning, training algorithms, language models, reinforcement learning and bandits, inverse problems, and sparse representation theory.
